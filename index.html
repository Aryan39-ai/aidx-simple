Projects

Open projects

Work a student could pick up now. Each one is a question we are trying to answer, not a topic area.

01

Can an agent be trusted with real tools?

Honours / Summer intern · Supervisor S. Aryan · Open

Give an agent a small set of real tools and a budget, then log every step to see where it goes wrong when nobody is checking.

You would build the sandbox and compare how two agents spend the same budget.

02

How far does a poisoned input travel?

Honours / PhD · Supervisor A. Arjun · Open

One bad input goes into a chain of agents that trust each other. We measure how many steps it survives, and what stops it.

You would write the test cases and turn the results into a dataset.

03

Does an explanation change the decision?

Honours · Supervisor S. Aryan · Open

People make the same decision with and without an AI explanation. We look at whether the explanation changes the choice, or only their confidence in it.

You would run the sessions and analyse the results.

How to apply

  1. 01Explore. Read the research areas.
  2. 02Choose. Pick one that interests you.

What happens after you email

We reply within about a week. If the fit looks right, we set up a 20-minute chat to talk through the project before anything formal starts. You will not be left wondering.

Method

Why this site looks like this

The structure came from a review of ten Australian university research-lab websites. Every choice below traces back to that review.

What the review foundWhat this site doesSeen in
Every lab is competing for students“Join the lab” is the only solid button on the page9 of 10 sites
Entry requirements were vague or missingA requirements table, with blanks where the number is not confirmed1 of 10 sites did this well
People listed as a name and a job titleEach person is written as a path, in their own wordsMelbourne
Past members deleted when they leaveAn outcomes table showing where they went nextCurtin
News pages that stopped years agoNo news page at all, so nothing can go stale4 of 10 sites had dead pages
